How to Play

  1. Win Condition: Be the first to score 50 points.
  2. Your Turn: Click "Roll Dice" to roll. The number on the dice gets added to your current score.
  3. Keep Rolling or Stop? You can roll again as many times as you want during your turn.
  4. The Risk: If you roll a 1, you lose your entire current score and your turn ends immediately.
  5. Save Your Score: Click "Hold" to add your current score to your total and end your turn. Now it's safe!
  6. The Other Player Plays: Players take turns rolling and holding.
  7. Win: The first player to reach exactly 50 points wins!
